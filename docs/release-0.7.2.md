# OmniDash 0.7.2

## PS5: the 2026 Home
- Category bar across the top: L1 · Plus · Store · Games · Library · Media · R1 (L1 / R1 step through it).
- Small game tiles with the selected game tall in the first slot and its name beside it.
- The game's key art fills the screen, with its logo, its own short description, **Play Game** and **…**.
- Activity Cards (Trophies, Friends Who Play with their avatars, Captures) peek up from the bottom.
- New open-licence font (Source Sans 3), redrawn icons, and PS5-style L1/R1/Options buttons.

## Simpler setup and Settings
- Onboarding is two screens: pick your dashboard (from real screenshots), then bring your games. **Change** sets your name and picture: your Steam picture, a gamerpic or your own.
- Settings is one short page in five groups. Full screen, the startup animation and notifications are now always on.

## Lighter and safer
- Electron 44 (from 39), clearing 24 security advisories; undici and fast-uri patched too.
- About 90 MB less memory at idle: audio no longer runs as a separate process.
- PS5 no longer uses the GPU while idle.
- Fewer PC tells: no browser tooltips, pinch zoom, middle-click scrolling, Tab focus jumps or spellcheck underlines.
