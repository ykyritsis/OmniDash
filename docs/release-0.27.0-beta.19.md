# 0.27.0-beta.19

Search, support and a quieter launcher.

- **Search everything at once.** The search panel finds games in your library, then the Xbox Store and the Steam Store, with prices. Store results open the same game pages as the Store. It's restyled to match the rest of the launcher.
- **Friends' activity.** A list of your Steam friends under Play with friends: playing first, then online, busy and away.
- **Support & backup in Settings → Experience.** Copy diagnostics (version, start-up state, the last launch and recent activity, with keys and your user folder removed). Export settings to one file, with API keys left out unless you choose to include them. Restore settings from a backup.
- **Safe mode.** If the launcher fails to start properly twice in a row, the next start runs with background refresh, prefetch and update checks paused.
- **Cancel while launching.** The launch screen has a Cancel button, and Steam's start-up wait can be cancelled too.
- **Quieter artwork progress.** It's a small pill at the bottom centre with a hairline, not a card in the corner.
- **New launcher icon.** The Xbox mark in blue on a dark tile, used for the window, the exe and the installer.

Downloads: `Xbox-Preview-UI-Setup-0.27.0-beta.19.exe` (installer) and `Xbox-Preview-UI-Portable-0.27.0-beta.19.exe`, with checksums in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The builds aren't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run. Beta 18 and later update themselves.
