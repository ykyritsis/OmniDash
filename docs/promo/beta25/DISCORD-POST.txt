Xbox Preview UI — beta 25 preview 🎮

I've been building a console-style home for PC games, designed around a controller and Windows handhelds.

• Bring Steam, Epic, Xbox PC and other installed games into one library.
• Jump back into games from a full-screen home dashboard.
• Choose supported Steam launch modes inside the app, including Advanced Warfare's multiplayer / Exo options.
• Follow launch and first-run setup status without guessing what's happening.
• Browse with a controller, mouse or keyboard.

Beta 25 cleans up the home cards and selection corners, and replaces the old custom keyboard with the native Windows touch keyboard throughout the app.

The images show beta 25 with a curated demo library. This is an unofficial community project for Windows.

Project + available builds:
https://github.com/ykyritsis/xbox-cloud-preview-on-pc

I'd love feedback from other handheld users — especially on navigation, scaling and native keyboard behaviour.
