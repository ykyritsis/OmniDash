EDITABLE SOURCE
artboards.html — exact text, layout, image placements and colours.
Open with #hero, #square, #social, #modes or #wordmark to select a layout.
scripts/promo-render.cjs exports all five layouts using Electron.
scripts/promo-capture.cjs captures the app using cached local demo assets.
Capture inputs live under .visual-review and are not distributed in this kit.
PROMPTS.txt records the image-generation prompts and method.
DISCORD-POST.txt includes long and short copy for manual posting.
